Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CarDoc AI handles information when you use the CarDoc AI mobile application and related features.

1. Information CarDoc AI may process

CarDoc AI is an automotive diagnostic application. Depending on the vehicle, adapter, device permissions, and features you use, the app may process:

  • Vehicle diagnostic data, such as diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), freeze-frame data, live sensor values, supported PID information, ECU/module identifiers, protocol information, and vehicle health results.
  • Vehicle identification information, such as VIN, make, model, model year, ECU identifiers, calibration identifiers, and related vehicle metadata when available.
  • OBD adapter information, such as adapter type, connection method, protocol, latency, quality score, and connection status.
  • App-generated diagnostic information, such as scan history, AI diagnostic summaries, sensor-health results, repair-verification results, and test reports.
  • App preferences and local settings, such as selected dashboard items, compatibility mode, last successful adapter, and other settings needed to improve app usability.

2. Bluetooth, Nearby Devices, Wi-Fi and USB

CarDoc AI may request access to Bluetooth / Nearby Devices and may use Wi-Fi or USB connectivity in order to discover and connect to compatible OBD adapters. These permissions are used for vehicle diagnostics and adapter connectivity.

CarDoc AI does not use Bluetooth or Nearby Devices permissions for advertising or unrelated tracking.

3. VIN lookup and internet services

When online VIN identification is used, CarDoc AI may send the vehicle's VIN to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) vPIC service in order to retrieve make, model, year, and related vehicle information.

Important: When an online VIN lookup is performed, the VIN is transmitted to the external VIN lookup service for that purpose.

NHTSA vPIC is operated by the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration. You can learn more at https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/.

4. Local storage

CarDoc AI may store certain information locally on your device, including app settings, scan history, dashboard preferences, performance-test history, and diagnostic reports. This information is used to provide app functionality and improve continuity between sessions.

You can generally remove locally stored app information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app from your device.

5. Reports and sharing

CarDoc AI may allow you to export or share diagnostic reports, CSV files, or other diagnostic information. CarDoc AI does not automatically share these reports with other people. Information is shared only when you choose to export or share it.

6. Payments and subscriptions

CarDoc AI may offer paid features, subscriptions, or professional plans through Google Play. Purchases and payment processing are handled by Google Play. CarDoc AI does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Google may process purchase-related information according to Google's own privacy policies.

7. Advertising

CarDoc AI does not currently use third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell personal information for advertising purposes.

8. Analytics and crash reporting

The current version of CarDoc AI does not intentionally collect third-party advertising analytics. If analytics or crash-reporting services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosure will be updated before release.

9. How information is used

Information processed by CarDoc AI may be used to:

  • connect to an OBD adapter and communicate with supported vehicle systems;
  • display diagnostic trouble codes and live vehicle data;
  • identify a vehicle or ECU when the necessary information is available;
  • generate diagnostic summaries, health scores, sensor-health results, and reports;
  • restore app preferences and reconnect to previously used adapters;
  • provide paid or professional features purchased through Google Play.

10. Data selling and disclosure

CarDoc AI does not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to a service provider only when needed to provide a feature you request, such as online VIN lookup or Google Play billing. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

11. Data security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Automotive safety

CarDoc AI is a diagnostic tool and does not replace professional inspection or manufacturer service information. Diagnostic values can vary by vehicle, adapter, ECU, protocol, and operating condition.

Write, coding, adaptation, or service functions—when supported—should only be used on compatible vehicles and with appropriate service procedures.

13. Children's privacy

CarDoc AI is not designed specifically for children. The app is intended for vehicle owners, drivers, technicians, and automotive professionals.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as CarDoc AI adds or changes features, services, permissions, or data practices. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
